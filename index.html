Circuit Model of Stimulus-Specific Adaptation in Auditory Cortex

Mathematical and Computational Neuroscience (MATH 568), 2026

Neurons in auditory cortex respond less to a repeated tone and more to a rare one. This project asks how much of that effect comes from depression at thalamocortical synapses and how much from short-term plasticity at interneuron synapses, by fitting two versions of a cortical circuit model to oddball recordings from rat auditory cortex.

Cochlear Implant Speech Processing and Intelligibility

Digital Signal Processing (EECS 351), 2026

A cochlear-implant-style processing pipeline in Python, with a noise vocoder to resynthesize what the processed speech sounds like. The project measures how channel count, envelope cutoff, and compression change intelligibility, and compares an objective intelligibility metric against automatic speech recognition.

Write-up, audio demos, and code coming soon

Thalamocortical Network Model of Absence Seizures

Team project, Mathematical Models (MATH 462), 2025

A 350-neuron conductance-based network of cortical and thalamic cells. Strengthening corticothalamic feedback moves the network from gamma oscillations to 3 Hz seizure-like discharges, and a fast–slow analysis explains how slow inhibition sets the discharge period.
