I'm an undergraduate at the University of Michigan pursuing a B.S.E. in Electrical Engineering and a B.S. in Biopsychology, Cognition, and Neuroscience and Mathematics, expected July 2027.

I study how the auditory system processes sound, with a particular interest in mechanistic models grounded in neural physiology. I currently work with Anahita Mehta at the Kresge Hearing Research Institute, using EEG to study how listeners respond when a chord breaks harmonic expectation. Previously I worked with Gideon Rothschild on sound-guided navigation in rats.
